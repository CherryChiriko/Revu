To generate an APK:
npm run build
npx cap sync android
cd android
.\gradlew.bat assembleDebug
cd ..


How to install it on your phone

    Transfer the app-debug.apk file to your phone (via USB, Google Drive, WhatsApp, or email).

    Tap the file on your phone to open it.

    If prompted, toggle on "Allow installation from unknown sources" in your phone settings.

    Tap Install, and Revu will show up on your app drawer like any standard app from the Google Play Store!

    Note: Debug APKs are meant for personal testing. When you're ready to publish to the Google Play Store later, you'll generate a signed release file (.aab / Signed APK) instead, which requires a developer keystore.
